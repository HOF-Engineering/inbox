# HOF Pack — WhatsApp Templates (US number)

Rewritten to sound human and to lean toward **Utility** category (needed for reliable US delivery).
Each template is anchored to a real action (inquiry / phone call / quotation) — that anchor is what
keeps it out of Marketing.

**Variables:** `{{1}}` = client first name · `{{2}}` = agent name · `{{3}}` = product (where used)
**Language:** English (US) — `en_US`

---

## 1. Box details request  ·  name: `pack_box_details_request`  ·  Category: **Utility** ✅

Hi {{1}}, this is {{2}} from HOF Pack. Thanks for your inquiry about custom {{3}} boxes.

To put together an accurate quote, could you share a few details:
- Box size (Length × Width × Height)
- Quantity you need
- Printing requirements (logo, artwork, or design)

Once I have these, I'll prepare your quotation and send it over.

*Sample values: 1=John, 2=Steve, 3=cosmetic*

## Category notes (important for US delivery)

- **Solid Utility (safe): #1, #2, #5** — each tied to a concrete transaction (inquiry, call, quote). These
  should get Utility and deliver to US recipients.
- **Borderline: #3, #4** — reframed to reference the inquiry/quote to improve Utility odds, but Meta may
  still flip a light "follow-up/intro" to Marketing. After submitting, check the assigned category.
- **Avoid (turns Marketing):** pure greetings ("Hi, hope you're well!"), promo words (offer, discount,
  best, exclusive), generic "just checking in" with no inquiry/quote reference.
- When submitting, **select Utility** and use the sample values above.
